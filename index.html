THE IDEA

Explainable AI for
Citrus Disease Diagnosis

Citrus fruit diseases can affect quality, yield, and a grower's livelihood. This project presents an approachable way to explore image-based diagnosis alongside the reasons, visible symptoms, potential impact, and prevention guidance behind each result.

HOW IT WORKS

A result should come with a reason.

The connected classifier evaluates citrus leaf photos and returns its top class scores. Its prediction is a clue to investigate, not proof or a lab diagnosis.
